What is Preggo.AI?

Preggo.AI is a conversational assistant built with Google's CX Agent Studio. It answers everyday pregnancy questions in plain, friendly language.

📚

General information

Learn about pregnancy stages, common symptoms, nutrition and wellbeing.

🕒

Available anytime

Ask questions whenever they come up, day or night.

🩺

Points you to care

Encourages you to speak with a doctor or midwife for anything personal or urgent.

How to use it

  1. Open the chat using the chat window or bubble on this page.
  2. Ask a question in your own words, e.g. “What foods should I avoid?”
  3. Check with a professional before acting on anything important.

⚠️ Prototype & privacy notice

  • This is a student prototype made for an SMU course project. It is not a medical device or a commercial service.
  • Not medical advice. Answers are general and may be incomplete or wrong. Always consult a qualified doctor, midwife or pharmacist about your own health.
  • In an emergency (e.g. heavy bleeding, severe pain, reduced baby movements), call 995 or go to the nearest A&E immediately.
  • This website does not store your conversations. Messages are sent to and processed by Google's CX Agent Studio service, which powers the chatbot.
  • Please don't share personal details such as your full name, NRIC, contact details or medical records.
