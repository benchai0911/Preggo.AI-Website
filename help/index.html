Help and support

MamaFirst.AI can listen, but some moments need a real person. Tap a number to call.

Emergencies: 995

For you or your baby: heavy bleeding, severe pain, trouble breathing, baby moving much less than usual, or any time you feel in danger.

Call 995

Mindline: 1771

Feeling low, anxious or not like yourself? Talk to a trained counsellor on Singapore's national mindline. Free, private, open 24 hours.

Call 1771

MamaFirst.AI is a companion, not a doctor. It does not give medical advice.
